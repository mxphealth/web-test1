How the partnership process may work

The exact process will depend on participating manufacturers, buyer qualifications, product requirements, and the destination market.

1

Understand the opportunity

A manufacturer or potential buyer shares its product interests, target country, business profile, and supply expectations.

2

Identify a suitable match

Where a suitable opportunity exists, MaxproHealth may help introduce the parties and coordinate initial business discussions.

3

Discuss wholesale terms directly

The manufacturer and buyer can review product selection, pricing, minimum order quantity (MOQ), payment, shipping, and repeat-order terms.

4

Confirm the arrangement in writing

Any distribution authorization, exclusivity, labeling, order obligations, and other commercial terms must be agreed by the relevant parties before being represented as confirmed.

MaxproHealth's proposed role: sourcing support, introductions, and business communication. Unless separately agreed in writing, MaxproHealth is not the manufacturer, importer of record, product certifier, or seller of the products, and does not guarantee that a partnership or order will be completed.

Local distribution and labeling opportunities

One idea for future manufacturer discussions is to make it easier for an approved local partner to distribute products in its market while preserving the original brand identity.

01

Local distributor identification

Where the manufacturer agrees and local rules allow it, packaging may identify the approved local distributor or importer, for example, “Distributed by [Company Name], [Country].”

02

Manufacturer-approved orders

MOQ, mixed-product orders, wholesale pricing, and delivery terms should be confirmed with each manufacturer. Small-business options depend on the supplier's capabilities.

03

Country-specific compliance

Any relabeling, language requirements, importer details, product registration, and claims must be reviewed for the destination market before products are supplied.

The labeling and distribution concept is preliminary. It is not a claim that any manufacturer has approved it or that any product currently has a local distributor.
